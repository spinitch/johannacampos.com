MY NORTH STAR
Simpler products mean less time on a screen, more time being human. That’s what I design toward, for a Fortune 500 platform or a startup’s first release.
JOHANNA CAMPOS

Most products keep you scrolling, either on purpose or by accident, through friction and confusion dressed up as features. I design the opposite way; simple enough that you get what you need and move on. Good design isn’t just about making things look pretty. It’s about making things simple to use, and delightful when you use them. That’s one of the most underused business levers there is, and it’s the standard I hold every project to, mine included.
